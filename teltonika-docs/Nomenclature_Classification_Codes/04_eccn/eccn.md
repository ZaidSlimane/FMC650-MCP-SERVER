# FMC650 ECCN

An **Export Control Classification Number** (**ECCN**) is an alphanumeric code assigned to articles, technology and software (collectively, "items") by the United States Department of Commerce, Bureau of Industry and Security.

*FMC650* ECCN - **7A994**
